Proficiency Testing Uptake Accelerator (PTUA)

A simpler, more affordable way to access proficiency testing.

PTUA is established as a single, coordinated ordering mechanism for proficiency testing (PT) across African countries.

Pre-launch: participating PT providers, schemes, prices and enrollment arrangements are being finalized.

Why PTUA?

Regular proficiency testing is often hard to arrange and costly. PTUA is designed to change both.

One place to order

One mechanism instead of separate arrangements with each provider.

Lower costs

Preferential prices through pooled demand.

An open market

For public and private laboratories and testing sites, at all tiers of the health system.

Proficiency testing shows laboratories and testing sites where corrective action is needed, so they can improve the quality of the diagnostic services delivered to patients.

Who can participate

Built for laboratory networks and testing sites across Africa

PTUA is available to public and private laboratories and testing sites in all countries in Africa. National disease programmes, laboratory directorates, laboratory networks and individual testing sites can all help identify where expanded PT participation is needed.

National disease programmes

Identify priority gaps in PT coverage across disease testing networks and consider how regular participation can be financed.

Laboratory directors

Map which laboratories and testing sites need PT and which testing methods require external quality assessment.

Laboratorians and testing-site managers

Identify the tests performed at your site for which PT participation would support quality improvement.

Detailed enrollment, financing and scheme eligibility conditions will be communicated before orders open.

Accredited PT

PT schemes

The PTUA is working with African providers to make ISO/IEC 17043 accredited schemes available for the following areas and more.

  • HIV testing, for example serology and viral load
  • Tuberculosis, for example microscopy, molecular testing, culture and drug-susceptibility testing
  • Malaria, for example microscopy and rapid diagnostic tests
  • Bacteriology and antimicrobial resistance testing
  • Selected serology, including hepatitis and syphilis
CatalogueA catalogue of ISO/IEC 17043 accredited schemes will be made available early in 2027.
How it will work

One mechanism, with transparent pricing before enrollment

PTUA is designed to simplify how laboratory networks and testing sites procure PT.

  1. 01

    Identify PT needs

    Laboratory networks, programmes and testing sites identify the testing areas and locations that would benefit from PT.

  2. 02

    Review the available offer

    Once the catalogue is confirmed, participating buyers will be able to review relevant schemes and their prices, including any applicable PTUA financial support.

  3. 03

    Coordinate participation through PTUA

    Orders will be organized through the PTUA mechanism, with the applicable payment, provider and participation arrangements communicated before commitment.

Affordability. PTUA is designed to lower the cost of participation through preferential pricing.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is PTUA?

A coordinated mechanism that makes it easier and more affordable for laboratory networks and testing sites to access proficiency testing from African providers.

Who is PTUA intended for?

Public and private laboratories and testing sites, including national, regional and peripheral networks, in all African countries. Detailed enrollment conditions will be shared before participation opens.

Will PTUA make PT cheaper?

PTUA is designed to reduce costs through pooled demand, preferential pricing and a time-limited upfront subsidy for eligible participation. Final scheme prices, subsidy levels and terms are not yet available.

Which PT schemes will be available?

The PTUA is working with African providers to make ISO/IEC 17043 accredited schemes available for the areas listed above and more. A catalogue of accredited schemes will be made available early in 2027.

Will schemes be accredited?

PTUA prioritizes qualified African PT providers and internationally recognized quality standards. Accreditation details, including the ISO/IEC 17043 status and relevant scope of each scheme, will be presented when the catalogue is finalized.

When can we place orders?

Enrollment and ordering arrangements are being finalized. The website will be updated when the mechanism is ready and ordering dates have been confirmed.

Can Global Fund grant financing be used?

Countries may consider approved Global Fund grant resources, domestic funding or other eligible sources for their contribution, subject to the relevant budget authorizations and financing requirements. This does not imply automatic financing approval.

What should we do now?

Identify the laboratories and testing sites that need PT and the testing areas they cover, and follow PTUA launch and enrollment updates.
